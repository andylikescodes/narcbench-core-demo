## 7. Readings per model (rules of design section 6)

### Qwen3-32B (γ-scaled frames, E1)

- **R1_in_distribution**: J-space separates colluders in distribution
- **R2_transfer**: mixed: C minus J clear, J vs random unclear, complement vs full differs
- **R3_complement_uninformative**: complement-of-random equals full (as expected); 'complement equals full' says nothing about J-space by itself
- **R4_within_transfer**: intermediate: within-Transfer J probe above chance but below 0.80, or Core-fit J above 0.65
- **R5_phase_vs_family**: the J drop is mostly the phase change (discussion to final)
- **R6_dimension**: dimension is not the explanation: a supervised or PCA subspace of at most 16 dimensions reaches the full transfer AUROC while J does not

### DeepSeek-R1-32B (γ-scaled frames, E1)

- **R1_in_distribution**: J-space separates colluders in distribution
- **R2_transfer**: the transferable signal is not in J-space
- **R3_complement_uninformative**: complement-of-random equals full (as expected); 'complement equals full' says nothing about J-space by itself
- **R4_within_transfer**: role information is absent from J-space on Transfer finals (within-Transfer J probe at its permutation null)
- **R5_phase_vs_family**: the J drop is mostly the phase change (discussion to final)
- **R6_dimension**: small supervised/PCA subspaces do not reach the full probe either; dimension may matter

### GPT-OSS-20B (γ-scaled frames, E1)

- **R1_in_distribution**: in distribution J-space is a weaker carrier than the full residual
- **R2_transfer**: J-space and its complement are not clearly separated on Transfer (condition i fails)
- **R3_complement_uninformative**: complement-of-random equals full (as expected); 'complement equals full' says nothing about J-space by itself
- **R4_within_transfer**: intermediate: within-Transfer J probe above chance but below 0.80, or Core-fit J above 0.65
- **R5_phase_vs_family**: the J drop is shared between phase and family
- **R6_dimension**: small supervised/PCA subspaces do not reach the full probe either; dimension may matter
