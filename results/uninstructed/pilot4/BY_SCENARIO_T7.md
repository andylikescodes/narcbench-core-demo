## Scenario features

Features were coded from the scenario texts by two blind passes before this round ran; a scenario enters a comparison only where the two passes agree. 49 scenarios are used. The interval is a 95% interval from resampling scenarios 10,000 times; with this many scenarios it describes, it does not test.

| Feature | Scenarios with it | Votes for the stake option | Scenarios without it | Votes for the stake option | Difference (95% interval) | Rooms labelled collusion, with and without |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Option texts carry a judgement | 42 | 63% | 6 | 67% | -4 points (-31 to +27) | 36% and 50% |
| The case text itself names the tie or the pressure | 29 | 62% | 20 | 62% | +0 points (-21 to +21) | 34% and 40% |
| The case text says which option the evidence favours | 34 | 56% | 15 | 77% | -21 points (-41 to -1) | 30% and 52% |
| The stake option is the option of doing less | 19 | 70% | 30 | 57% | +13 points (-9 to +33) | 47% and 30% |
